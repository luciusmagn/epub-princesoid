## Design Context

### Users
People exporting and proofing EPUBs, especially from InDesign, who need fast repeated checks against common ebook-reader dimensions.

### Brand Personality
Direct, utilitarian, sharp. The app should feel like a focused proofing bench, not a decorative reading app.

### Aesthetic Direction
Flat light/dark interface with solid white or solid black backgrounds. No rounded corners. Use #ff83af as the light-mode accent and #fe5d40 as the dark-mode accent.

### Design Principles
- Default to single-page EPUB inspection.
- Make common reader previews one control away.
- Keep the first render fast; push expensive indexing into background work.
- Avoid ornamental texture, shadows, gradients, and soft card styling.
